B2B隐私协议说明

浙江伟丰新材料有限公司（以下简称“伟丰”“我们”或“本公司”）高度重视商业合作中的个人信息安全。本协议说明我们在客户服务、供应商管理、采购销售、项目合作和其他企业对企业（B2B）业务中，如何处理相关企业联系人、授权代表、工作人员及其他业务相关人员的个人信息。

请您在向我们提供个人信息前仔细阅读本协议。如果您代表所在企业向我们提供其他人员的信息，请确保您已获得合法授权，并已向相关人员说明本协议内容。普通网站浏览场景同时适用我们的《隐私政策》。

01 适用范围

本协议适用于我们在供应商考察与准入、客户咨询与关系维护、报价与样品、采购与销售、招投标、合同谈判和履行、订单与物流、质量管理、项目实施、技术服务、会议展会、商务拜访、审计合规以及企业场所和信息系统访问等B2B场景中的个人信息处理活动。

相关人员包括客户、供应商、经销商、承包商、服务提供商及合作伙伴的员工、联系人和授权代表，外部顾问、专家、访客，以及因业务需要与我们建立联系的其他人员。本协议不适用于本公司员工的人力资源管理活动。

02 我们可能处理的信息类型

类别可能涉及的信息典型场景
身份与联系信息姓名、企业名称、部门、职务、工作地址、工作邮箱、办公电话、手机号、微信号建立业务联系、发送资料、会议安排和客户服务。
业务与交易信息询价和报价、样品申请、采购或销售订单、合同及履约记录、项目沟通、发票与收付款信息、物流信息、售后和投诉记录合同磋商、订单交付、对账结算、质量跟踪与争议处理。
资质与合规信息授权文件、签名或印章影像、从业资格、企业资质材料中的联系人信息、尽职调查或供应商评估资料核验代表权限、供应商准入、审计及合规管理。
访问与技术信息IP地址、浏览器与设备信息、访问时间、系统操作和安全日志企业网站或经授权的业务系统访问、安全防护和故障排查。
场所安全信息访客登记、单位、联系电话、车牌、进出时间、门禁或视频监控记录您访问厂区、办公场所或参加现场活动时的安全管理。

身份证件号码、个人银行账户、签名影像、精确位置以及特定场景下的访客影像等可能构成敏感个人信息。我们仅会在具有特定目的和充分必要性时处理，并依法履行单独告知、取得单独同意或采取其他必要措施。企业账户、纯企业经营数据和匿名化信息本身不属于个人信息，但其中包含可识别自然人的内容时，本协议仍然适用。

03 个人信息来源

我们可能直接从您处获取信息，也可能从您所在企业或其授权人员、我们的业务合作伙伴和服务提供商、依法公开的企业网站或行业名录、会议展会及公开商业渠道、公司网站和业务通讯工具，以及依法有权提供信息的监管部门或行业机构处获得信息。

从第三方获取信息时，我们会要求信息来源合法，并在法律要求的范围内向您告知相关情况。

04 处理目的与处理依据

业务运营与合同管理

用于客户和供应商建档、资质审核、报价、样品、采购销售、订单、合同、交付、对账、售后及项目管理。处理依据可能包括取得您的同意，为订立或履行您作为一方当事人的合同所必需，或法律法规规定的其他依据。

日常沟通与关系维护

用于回复咨询、安排会议或拜访、发送技术和产品资料、处理反馈并维护业务关系。我们将基于您的主动请求、同意，或在合理范围内处理您自行公开或其他已经合法公开的信息。

质量、安全与合规

用于产品质量追溯、供应链管理、网络和场所安全、反欺诈、反商业贿赂、审计、争议处理以及响应监管要求。处理依据可能包括履行法定义务、应对紧急情况、保护自然人生命健康和财产安全，或法律法规允许的其他情形。

业务资讯与活动邀请

在取得所需同意或法律允许时，我们可能向企业联系人发送与其职务相关的产品资讯、行业活动或调研邀请。您可随时通过信息中提供的方式或联系我们拒绝后续推送。

05 个人信息的共享、转让与公开披露

我们不会出售个人信息。为实现前述目的且具有合法依据时，我们可能向关联公司、网站和信息技术服务商、通信与会议服务商、物流仓储服务商、金融机构、检验认证或审计机构、专业顾问，以及依法有权的政府和监管部门提供必要信息。

我们会遵循最小必要原则，要求受托处理方按照我们的指示和约定处理信息，并采取相应保护措施。发生合并、重组、资产转让、破产清算等业务变更时，我们将依法告知接收方信息并要求其继续履行保护义务。除取得单独同意或法律法规另有规定外，我们不会公开披露您的个人信息。

06 跨境传输

伟丰的日常经营主要位于中国境内。因国际客户服务、跨境项目、境外参展、国际物流或境外服务商支持等业务确需向境外提供个人信息时，我们将依法告知境外接收方、处理目的、方式和信息种类，并根据适用法律取得单独同意、开展安全评估、签订标准合同或采取其他必要保护措施。

07 保存期限

我们仅在实现处理目的所需的最短期限内保存个人信息。确定期限时会考虑业务关系持续时间、合同履行及质保需求、税务财务和档案要求、诉讼时效、安全审计以及争议处理等因素。期限届满后，我们将依法删除或匿名化处理相关信息；法律法规要求继续保存的，我们会限制其进一步使用。

08 安全措施

我们采取与风险相适应的组织、技术和物理措施，包括分级分类与最小化、访问权限控制、身份验证、传输或存储保护、日志与备份管理、员工保密和培训、供应商安全管理、漏洞修复及安全事件响应等，防止信息遭到未经授权的访问、使用、披露、篡改、损坏或丢失。

如发生可能危害个人权益的安全事件，我们将及时采取补救措施，并依法履行通知和报告义务。

09 您的权利

在适用法律规定的范围内，您有权知情、决定、限制或拒绝处理，并可请求查阅、复制、更正、补充或删除个人信息，撤回同意，要求解释处理规则，或在符合法定条件时申请转移个人信息。

您可以通过本协议末尾的联系方式提出请求。为保护您和所在企业的安全，我们可能核验您的身份、授权或与相关业务的关联，并将在法律规定或合理期限内答复。

10 Cookie、本地存储与系统日志

本网站使用浏览器本地存储保存语言和主题偏好，并可能由服务器或第三方网页资源服务商生成必要的访问和安全日志。我们目前不使用广告Cookie，也不基于B2B联系人信息进行面向个人的广告画像。具体说明请参阅我们的《隐私政策》。

11 未成年人信息

我们的B2B业务面向依法设立的企业及其专业人员，并非面向不满十四周岁的未成年人。请不要代表未成年人向我们提供个人信息；如发现我们在没有有效授权的情况下处理了未成年人信息，我们将依法尽快删除或采取其他必要措施。

12 本协议的更新

我们可能根据业务、技术或法律法规变化更新本协议。最新版本将在本页面发布并注明日期。发生对个人权益有重大影响的变更时，我们会依法通过显著页面提示、业务联系或其他适当方式通知相关人员。

01 Scope

This Policy applies to supplier review and onboarding, customer enquiries and relationship management, quotations and samples, procurement and sales, tenders, contract negotiation and performance, orders and logistics, quality management, project delivery, technical support, meetings and exhibitions, business visits, audit and compliance, and access to company premises or authorized systems.

It covers personnel, contacts and representatives of customers, suppliers, distributors, contractors, service providers and partners; external advisers, experts and visitors; and others who interact with us for business purposes. It does not cover our employee human-resources processing.

03 Sources

We may receive information directly from you; your organization or its authorized personnel; our partners and service providers; lawful public sources such as corporate websites, industry directories, exhibitions and public commercial channels; our website and business communication tools; or regulators and industry bodies legally entitled to provide it.

Where information comes from a third party, we require a lawful source and provide notice where required.

04 Purposes and legal bases

Operations and contracts

Customer and supplier records, qualification checks, quotations, samples, procurement, sales, orders, contracts, delivery, settlement, support and projects. Legal bases may include consent, necessity to enter into or perform a contract with you, and other bases permitted by law.

Communications and relationships

Responding to enquiries, arranging meetings and visits, providing technical or product materials and handling feedback. We rely on your request or consent, or process lawfully disclosed information within a reasonable scope.

Quality, security and compliance

Quality traceability, supply-chain governance, network and premises security, fraud prevention, anti-bribery, audits, disputes and regulatory responses. Bases may include legal obligations, emergencies, protection of life, health or property, and other lawful grounds.

Business news and invitations

Where consent or law permits, we may send role-relevant product updates, event invitations or surveys. You can opt out through the message or by contacting us.

05 Sharing, transfer and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Where lawful and necessary, we may provide limited information to affiliates, website and IT providers, communications and meeting providers, logistics and warehousing providers, financial institutions, testing, certification or audit bodies, professional advisers, and competent authorities.

We apply data minimization, require processors to follow our instructions and agreements, and require appropriate safeguards. In a merger, restructuring, asset transfer or liquidation, we will provide legally required notice and require continued protection. We do not publicly disclose personal information without separate consent unless otherwise permitted by law.

06 International transfers

Wei Feng operates primarily in China. Where international customer support, cross-border projects, overseas exhibitions, international logistics or overseas service support requires information to be provided abroad, we will give legally required notice and use applicable safeguards, which may include separate consent, a security assessment, standard contract or other required measure.

07 Retention

We retain personal information only for the shortest period needed for its purpose. Relevant factors include the business relationship, contract and warranty needs, tax, finance and recordkeeping requirements, limitation periods, security audits and disputes. We then delete or anonymize it; where law requires continued storage, further use will be restricted.

08 Security

We use proportionate organizational, technical and physical measures, including classification and minimization, access control, authentication, transmission or storage protection, logs and backups, confidentiality and training, supplier security, vulnerability remediation and incident response.

If an incident may harm personal rights, we will promptly take remedial action and make notifications or reports required by law.

09 Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information, access, a copy, correction, completion or deletion; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; request an explanation; or request transfer where legal conditions are met.

Use the details below to submit a request. To protect you and your organization, we may verify your identity, authorization and relationship to the matter, and will respond within the period required by law or within a reasonable time.

10 Cookies, local storage and logs

The website uses browser local storage for language and theme preferences, and necessary access or security logs may be generated by the server or third-party web-resource providers. We do not currently use advertising cookies or build personal advertising profiles from B2B contact data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

11 Children

Our B2B activities are for established organizations and professionals, not children under 14. Do not provide information about a child on their behalf. If we learn that we processed a child's information without valid authorization, we will delete it or take other measures required by law.

12 Updates

We may update this Policy as business, technology or law changes. The current version and date will be published here. Material changes affecting personal rights will be highlighted through a page notice, business communication or another legally appropriate channel.
