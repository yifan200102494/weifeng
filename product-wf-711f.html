01Features

  • Imparts water repellency and general oil repellency.
  • Good wash durability.
  • Stable processing with low sensitivity to residual dyes and auxiliaries.
  • Low shade change and little effect on handle.

03Applications

For water-repellent finishing of cotton, polyester, nylon and their blends.

04Directions

General water repellency

Dosage: 5-20 g/L.

Durable water repellency

Dosage: 20-60 g/L; crosslinking agent: 4-12 g/L. One dip, one nip; dry, then heat-set at 160-185°C for 30-90 seconds.

Bath preparation

Remove residual dyes and surfactants from the fabric. Conduct compatibility trials before combining with resins or softeners. Adjust bath pH to 5.0-7.0 with acetic or citric acid.

Adjust the process and dosage based on actual fabric trials.
