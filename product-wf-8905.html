01Features

  • Imparts a full, fine and smooth handle.
  • Good hydrophilicity.
  • Very low yellowing and shade change; suitable for white fabrics.
  • Good salt and alkali stability.
  • Compatible with softeners, softening flakes and other auxiliaries.
